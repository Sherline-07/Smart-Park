Automated Allocation Rules
  • Automatic Slot Allocation: The system identifies and allocates the next compatible available slot automatically. Manual assignment is prevented to avoid collisions.
  • Duplicate Protection: Vehicles already checked in cannot be re-entered until the active parking session is settled.
  • Timestamp Recording: Entry timestamp is recorded down to the exact second in MySQL.
OPERATOR TIP
After entry confirmation, hand over the assigned slot number slip to the vehicle driver.
Vehicle Check-Out & Billing
Billing & Pricing Policy
  • Duration Rounding Rule: First hour is charged as a full hour. Any additional fractional hour rounds up to the next full hour (e.g. 1 hr 10 min = 2 hrs; 3.5 hrs = 4 hrs).
  • Dynamic Surge Pricing: If total parking occupancy is > 80%, high occupancy rates apply (2W: ₹30/hr, 4W: ₹60/hr).
  • Slot Release: As soon as checkout completes, the assigned slot immediately transitions to AVAILABLE for new vehicles.
